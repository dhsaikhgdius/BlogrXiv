Eleven editorial shelves for technical AI writing. Classification follows the post’s primary mechanism, not the author’s institution or the news cycle.

How we classify

A taxonomy of mechanisms, not a news desk

Each indexed post is assigned one primary category. Neighbor shelves exist so that a VLM analysis does not sit next to a video diffusion recipe, and so that a PhD advising essay is not mixed with an LLM-as-judge methodology note. Lab homepages, product landings, and paper-only listings stay out of the index even when they are famous.

Foundation Model
Pretraining, datasets, architecture, and scaling. Not a launch page.
LLM & MLLM

Multimodal Model
VLMs, grounding, and fused perception — not pure image generators.
Visual Generation
Diffusion, flow matching, and controllable image or video synthesis.
World Model
Simulators, robotics/VLA, dynamics, and model-based planning.
AI Agents
Tools, harnesses, memory, multi-agent systems, and agent evaluation.
Efficient AI
Kernels, parallelism, quantization, and training or inference systems.
Trustworthy AI
Safety, alignment, interpretability, security evals, and control.
Research Craft
How ML science is done: evals, experiment design, data analysis.
Frontier
Individual reports and technically substantive frontier essays — not lab indexes.
How to Research
Becoming a researcher: advising, taste, reading, writing, talks, PhD practice.

Multimodal Model

Vision-language, audio-language, document, and unified models that fuse perception with language. Posts should explain training recipes, grounding, evaluation, or failure modes — not a model card.

Boundary: image/video generators belong in Visual Generation; tool-using multimodal agents belong in AI Agents.

Visual Generation

Diffusion, flow matching, latent modeling, and controllable image or video synthesis. Useful posts discuss sampling, evaluation, infrastructure, or the geometry of the generative process.

Boundary: VLM question answering is Multimodal Model; learned video worlds used for planning are World Model.

World Model

Learned simulators, tactile and spatial representations, robotics/VLA, and model-based planning. The post should treat dynamics or embodiment as the object of study.

Boundary: perception-only VLMs stay in Multimodal Model; language agents that call tools stay in AI Agents.

AI Agents

Tool use, coding and browser harnesses, memory, multi-agent workflows, and agent evaluation. Infrastructure noise, causal evals, and long-horizon design notes belong here when the agent loop is the subject.

Boundary: model-only reasoning analyses are LLM & MLLM; embodied policy learning is World Model.

LLM & MLLM

Language and multimodal language models as objects of analysis: reasoning, mixture-of-experts, retrieval, instruction tuning, and post-training behavior. Prefer mechanistic or empirical essays over release notes.

Boundary: pretraining corpora and scaling laws are Foundation Model; fused perception systems are Multimodal Model.

Explore LLM & MLLM Blogs

Foundation Model

Pretraining mixes, datasets, architectures, training dynamics, and open-weight recipes. A post belongs here when the foundation stack itself — data, scale, or stability — is the contribution.

Boundary: capability scorecards and compute/power reports are Frontier; inference kernels are Efficient AI.

Pretraining Datasets Architecture Scaling Open Weights
Explore Foundation Model Blogs

Efficient AI

Attention kernels, parallelism, quantization, speculative decoding, and serving. The post should make a systems claim a practitioner can check against rooflines, traces, or ablations.

Boundary: algorithm-only architecture essays without a systems thesis stay in Foundation Model or LLM & MLLM.

Explore Efficient AI Blogs

Trustworthy AI

Alignment, interpretability, control evaluations, security, and monitoring. Include red-team methods and negative results when they change how a technical reader judges a claim.

Boundary: eval methodology without a safety thesis is Research Craft; agent harness design without a risk claim is AI Agents.

Alignment Interpretability Control AI Security Monitoring

Research Craft

How ML science is practiced: evaluation design, experiment hygiene, data traps, LLM-as-judge, and reproducibility as a method. These posts teach the craft of getting a trustworthy result.

Boundary: PhD advising, taste, and career essays belong in How to Research.

Evaluation Experiment Design Reproducibility LLM-as-Judge Data Analysis
Explore Research Craft Blogs

Frontier

Individual frontier reports and technically substantive essays on capability, compute, power, and deployment. Lab homepages, news indexes, and GitHub org pages are not Frontier posts.

Boundary: a training-recipe essay is Foundation Model; a safety eval is Trustworthy AI even if the model is frontier-scale.

How to Research

Advising, research taste, reading and writing, talks, and PhD practice. These essays are about becoming and remaining a researcher, not about a particular model.

Boundary: evaluation methodology and experiment checklists are Research Craft even when they are written as advice.
